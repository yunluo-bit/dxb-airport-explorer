V5 / 11 November 2026

The active cabin is newly authored THREE.js geometry in cabin-geometry.js, with rounded upholstered seats, seat-back displays, wood finishes, twin aisles, upper and main decks, open windows, overhead bins, curved ceilings, a forward stairway and a rear lounge. Repeated geometry uses InstancedMesh. Furniture textures are procedural, not photographs claimed to be scans.

Published reference configuration:
Emirates, 29 December 2020:
https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-takes-a380-experience-to-new-heights-unveils-premium-economy-plus-enhancements-across-all-cabins/
14 First Class + 76 Business + 56 Premium Economy + 338 Economy = 484.
Premium Economy 2-4-2; Economy 3-4-3; staggered Business 1-2-1; forward stairs; rear upper-deck lounge.
Counts and class zones are reference based. Row spacing, clearances, seat-shell shape, equipment placement, galley details and dimensions are approximated for this educational reconstruction. This is not an airline CAD model, a surveyed replica, or a booking map. Different Emirates A380 configurations exist.

Actual cabin reference photographs remain accessible from Real photo and the panel below the viewer. See V3-SOURCES.txt and V4-SOURCES.txt for the dated assets. The 2023 First Class photo and older Business wide photo are distinct references, not an assertion that all images show the same individual aircraft.

The real DXB apron photograph is now also used on distant scenery planes, visible through actual window openings. It is a 2D photographic backdrop, not a 360-degree scan or live view. See the existing Bernard Richardson / Condé Nast Traveler attribution. The cutaway fades the background while displaying the separated decks; the walking camera remains in the same 3D scene.

Exterior: original unpainted A380-omega A380-800 with Trent 900 geometry; GPL-2 licence and corresponding source retained at assets/v2/model/.
No FlyByWire asset was downloaded or incorporated. No paid asset was purchased.
THREE.js and its MIT licence remain locally vendored.

Existing V4 films and photo permissions are unchanged. No GitHub repository was created or modified and no website was published for V5.

    How to read this report

    Passenger profiles, English exercises and the sequence of teaching themes were designed for this report. They are not interviews or records of an actual transfer. Proposed arguments in scenes 13–14 are explicitly labelled drafts for group review.

    The 95.2 million figure refers to the year 2025. No live flight information, real gate assignment, walking distance or minimum transfer time is provided. Different locations and photographic moments are not presented as one continuous journey.

    Availability, access and commercial charges need confirmation with the airport, airline or facility. Airline-exclusive lounge access is not assumed. The Assisted Travel Lounge is in T2, separate from the T3 examples.

    Static text and real photographs remain available if the digital viewer cannot load. The map is official source material, with its edition and limits shown in the map scene.

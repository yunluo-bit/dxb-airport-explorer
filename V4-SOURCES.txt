MODEL AND COMPOSITING
Accurate existing A380-800 / Trent 900 exterior retained; original unpainted texture and community licence retained. A real airport background and transparent ground shadow replace the old opaque procedural apron. The camera is fitted for the foreground; free viewing is illustrative, not a calibrated photogrammetric reconstruction.
The opening effect clips the exterior and transitions into real photographic spaces. No low-poly individual seat blocks are shown in the finished cabin view. It is a photographic exploration, not a walkable full 3D interior.
The old model take-off is removed from the active page and active model module.
